Use your own photo

Pick any photo — a cartoon, a pet, anything — and it turns into black-and-white lines you can trace, all at once.

Point your phone camera down at a blank sheet of paper. The picture floats on top — follow the lines on the screen and draw them on the paper underneath. Prop the phone on a cup or stack of books so both hands stay free.
